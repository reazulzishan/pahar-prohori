# Pahar Prohori — Judge Demo

## 90-second route

1. **Mission Control (0–10 s)** — Point out the map-first interface: purple Relative Risk units, blue NISAR-change hatching and green ground nodes.
2. **Select Batali Hill · Sector 03 (10–20 s)** — Explain that `Relative Risk 0.84 / Critical` is susceptibility, while the current alert can still be `Normal`.
3. **Why this status? (20–30 s)** — Open the Evidence Chain to show Space → Environment → Terrain/AI → Ground → Movement → Decision.
4. **Run Monsoon Scenario (30–65 s)** — Let the real deterministic scenario progress: Normal → Watch → Warning → Evacuate. Do not seek the timeline during judging.
5. **Human action (65–75 s)** — At Evacuate, show the bilingual resident warning and the two-independent-signal rule.
6. **Replay (75–83 s)** — Show the July 2026 hindsight lock: acquisition is not the same as processed-product availability; post-event evidence cannot leak backward.
7. **Deployment (83–90 s)** — Change the node budget from 5 to 10 and click a proposed location. Explain that this is a prototype priority ranking, not an optimal deployment claim.

## One-line story

**Satellites know where. AI knows how likely. Ground sensors reveal when conditions are escalating.**

## Phrases to keep scientifically precise

- Say **Relative Risk Index**, not probability of failure.
- Say **illustrative NISAR change footprint** until GCOV processing is complete.
- Say **time-compressed simulated monsoon scenario**, not a real-time prediction.
- Say **prototype priority ranking**, not optimal placement.
- Say **validation pending** until the backtest metrics are actually measured.
